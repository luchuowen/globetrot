Vehicle import checker

Kenya registers right-hand-drive vehicles within an age limit. Under the 8-year rule, a vehicle imported in 2026 must have been first registered in 2019 or later.

This is a quick check, not a final decision. Special cases (for example, returning residents) have their own rules. Confirm with us before you buy.
