03Our shipping network

From international suppliers to Kenya and the region.

We coordinate cargo from China, Dubai, India, the UK and Europe into Kenya. Air freight typically arrives through JKIA, while sea freight uses the available port and shipping arrangements. After arrival, we coordinate customs clearance and onward delivery by road or rail where appropriate.

For regional shipments, our road and transit services connect Kenya with destinations along the Northern Corridor, including Uganda, Rwanda, South Sudan and the DRC.
