Who we are

Globetrot Cargolink International is a Nairobi-based freight forwarding and import logistics company. We help businesses and individuals bring goods into Kenya from China, Dubai, India, the UK and Europe, and move them on across East Africa.

We don't own ships or aircraft. We coordinate them. Our job is to plan the route, arrange the transport, handle the paperwork and keep you informed, so you deal with one team from your supplier's door to yours.

Importing usually means dealing with suppliers, shipping lines, airlines, customs and transporters separately. We bring it together.
