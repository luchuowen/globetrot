Air freight

Fast cargo into JKIA

For urgent or high-value goods that need to reach Kenya quickly. We coordinate express and consolidated air shipments arriving through Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (JKIA) in Nairobi, then arrange clearance and delivery.

Air cargo · JKIA

What's included

  • Express air freight for urgent shipments. Indicative transit from China is 2–7 days.
  • Consolidated air freight with weekly departures, so smaller shipments share space and cost.
  • Collection from your supplier and delivery in Nairobi and other parts of Kenya.
  • Customs clearance at JKIA, coordinated by our team.

How air freight is priced

1

Actual weightThe weight of your cargo in kilograms.

2

Volumetric weightLength × width × height in cm, divided by 6,000.

3

Chargeable weightWhichever of the two is higher. Try it in our CBM calculator.

Transit times are estimates and depend on flight schedules, handling and customs.

Sea freight

Containers into Mombasa

The most economical option for larger and heavier consignments. We coordinate container shipping into Mombasa, then arrange clearance and onward transport by rail or road.

Sea freight · Mombasa · Lamu

Shipping options

  • Full Container Load (FCL): a whole container for your goods. Sizes: 20-foot, 40-foot and 40-foot high-cube.
  • Less-than-Container Load (LCL): share container space with other shipments and pay by volume in cubic metres (CBM).
  • Roll-on/Roll-off (RoRo): for vehicles and machinery that are driven on and off the ship.
  • Onward transport from Mombasa, including rail to the Nairobi Inland Container Depot where applicable.

FCL or LCL?

S

Small volumesUsually LCL. You pay only for the space you use.

M

Medium volumesCompare the LCL price with a 20-foot container. We'll show you both.

L

Large volumesUsually FCL. Your goods travel sealed in their own container.

Indicative sea transit from China is about 22–30 days port to port. Sailing schedules, handling, customs and delivery affect the total time.

Customs clearance

Clearance and import documents

We help coordinate the documentation and clearance needed to import goods into Kenya, working with the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) and the relevant regulators. Expected duties and charges are explained before you pay.

iCMS · KenTrade

What we coordinate

  • Customs declarations through KRA's Integrated Customs Management System (iCMS).
  • Trade permits through KenTrade, Kenya's electronic single window, where required.
  • Harmonised System (HS) classification of your goods and guidance on the East African Community Common External Tariff (EAC CET).
  • Guidance on standards and certificates involving KEBS, KEPHIS and the Pharmacy and Poisons Board, including Pre-Export Verification of Conformity (PVoC) and Certificates of Conformity (CoC).

How clearance works

1

DocumentsWe review the invoice, packing list and bill of lading or air waybill.

2

EntryWe prepare and lodge the customs entry.

3

DutiesWe explain the expected duties and charges before payment.

4

ReleaseOnce cleared, cargo is released for delivery.

Requirements depend on your goods and the applicable regulations. Not every permit or certificate applies to every shipment.
