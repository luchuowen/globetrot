02Kenyan gateways

Where your cargo enters Kenya.

Most shipments arrive through one of these points. We coordinate clearance at the gateway and arrange the next leg to your door.

Sea gateway

Port of Mombasa

Kenya's main seaport for container, vehicle and bulk cargo arriving by sea.

Sea gateway

Lamu Port

A second deep-sea gateway on Kenya's north coast.

Air gateway

JKIA, Nairobi

Jomo Kenyatta International Airport handles air cargo into Nairobi.

Inland

Nairobi ICD

The Nairobi Inland Container Depot, linked to Mombasa by the Standard Gauge Railway (SGR).
