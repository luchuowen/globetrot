# Globetrot Cargolink: image and video prompts

These are written for the website layout. Every slot names where the text sits, so the subject is kept out of that area.

## House style (applies to every prompt)

- **Look:** editorial commercial photography. Premium logistics brand. Natural light, shallow depth of field. Shot on a full-frame camera with a 35mm or 50mm lens. Clean, uncluttered, no visible brand logos or readable text.
- **Palette:** deep harbour navy and blue-hour tones, with warm sodium-amber practical lights (#f3a43b). Cool shadows, warm highlights. No neon, no oversaturation.
- **People:** Kenyan scenes (Mombasa, Nairobi, JKIA, clearing, delivery, our office) use Black Kenyan / East African people. They are professional, confident and well dressed (smart casual or branded-free hi-vis). Origin scenes abroad (a China factory or a Dubai warehouse) may show local staff of that country.
- **Text-safe zone:** the area named in each slot stays calm and dark (sky, wall, blurred background) so white and amber text stays readable.
- **Output:** images are generated, then upscaled to 3840 px on the long edge. Videos are at least 1920×1080, 24–30 fps, 8–12 s, slow and steady camera, seamless loop where possible, no cuts.
- **Avoid:** logos, airline or shipping-line liveries, watermarks, readable text, extra fingers, warped hands, distorted containers, stock-photo grins.
