# Customer-first map: challenge -> how Globetrot solves it (31 Oct 2026)
Solutions limited to what globetrotcargolink.com states. No prices, days, duty %, guarantees.

1. "Where is my mzigo?" (supplier says "soon soon") -> client portal tracking; WhatsApp updates.
2. "What paperwork do I need?" (Certificate of Origin, invoices, permits) -> customs clearance: entries, documents, permits handled; import advisory before you ship.
3. "What will it really cost me?" (cheap on screen, surprises on arrival) -> duties and charges explained before payment; quote on WhatsApp.
4. "Sea or air?" -> sea (full and shared containers) and air via JKIA; advice on which fits the goods.
5. "Who delivers to my door?" -> delivery Nairobi, Mombasa and beyond; warehousing.
6. "I do not trust the process / fear of mistakes" -> one team from supplier to delivery; ask before you ship.
Rules: Dubai/Gulf and "consolidation" not used in posts; no "tracking every stage"; Certificate of Origin = most imports, some exempt, ask us.
