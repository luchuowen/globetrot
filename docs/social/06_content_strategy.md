# Content strategy
Status: RECOMMENDED, built on the accepted proposal (pillars, cadence, campaigns = CONFIRMED). Series names/KPIs below are NAVAC recommendations pending approval.

## Big idea (RECOMMENDED)
**"Importing into Kenya, made clear."** Globetrot becomes the account importers trust for plain-language, correct explanations — then quote with the people who explained it. Proof over promises.

## Pillars (CONFIRMED mix) with recurring series (RECOMMENDED)
| Pillar | Share | Signature series (repeatable, recognisable) |
|---|---|---|
| Import Made Simple | 35% | **Duty Decoded** (one cost line explained) · **Doc Desk** (document checklists) · **Myth vs Fact** |
| Services & Routes | 25% | **Lane Brief** (China/Dubai/India/UK → Kenya: ports, indicative transit, documents) · **Mode Match** (air vs sea) |
| Behind the Shipment | 15% | **Container Diaries** (a cargo's journey) · **Desk to Dock** (team at work) |
| Trust & Proof | 15% | **Four Commitments** · client stories (with written consent) · **Quote Teardown** (anatomy of an itemised quote) |
| Updates & Planning | 10% | **Notice Board** (regulatory/holiday/peak dates) |

## Formats
Mon graphic · Wed carousel · Fri 15–60 s video (proposal). Reuse each Friday video as Reel + TikTok + FB video + LinkedIn native video + WhatsApp Status. Add Stories weekly (proposal). Think beyond posters: motion route-maps, "cost stack" animations, document-walkthrough screen recordings, voice-led explainers (Christine voice plan: blueprint §6 — consent required), real warehouse/port footage when supplied.

## Every post must carry a brief (template)
`ID · Date · Platform(s) · Pillar/Series · Goal (awareness/trust/enquiry) · Audience · Hook (first 2 s/line) · Core message · CTA · Link+UTM · Sources/fact check · KPI to judge · Approver`
Hook rules: lead with the viewer's problem ("Your container landed. What do you owe?"). One idea per post.

## Fact safety (non-negotiable)
All duty rates, rules, transit times from blueprint §7 or an official source, with check date; label "indicative". Re-verify before posting anything tax/regulatory (Finance Act changes). No competitor naming. No guarantees. Client confirms technical/regulatory points (proposal duty). Never name the founder's former company.

## Engagement & community (RECOMMENDED; proposal says replies in business hours)
Reply to comments/DMs in business hours; target first reply ≤ 2 business hours (needs client approval before it is promised). Escalate quote requests to WhatsApp/form with a one-line summary. Weekly: comment on 10 relevant importer/logistics posts as the page; thank sharers; pin FAQ. Spam/abuse rules in 10.

## Lead generation & conversion
- Every post ends with one next step: WhatsApp / quote / guide.
- WhatsApp click-to-chat links with prefilled text per platform: `https://wa.me/254792330000?text=…` (format already used on site).
- UTM standard: `utm_source={platform}&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign={YYYY-MM-campaign}&utm_content={post-id}`. Website events (blueprint §2): quote_submit, whatsapp_click.
- `/links` link-in-bio page (in blueprint) with the same UTMs.
- Lead log: platform → enquiry → quote → won (shared sheet with Globetrot).
- Lead magnets: landed-cost estimator, CBM calculator, import checklist PDFs (site tools).

## Measurement
Baseline in Month 1 (Jan 2027); targets agreed with client in Month 1–2 — **none invented here**. Per-post KPI: Graphic = reach/saves; Carousel = saves/shares/clicks; Video = 3-s views, completion, shares; all = enquiries by UTM. Monthly report per proposal §13; quarterly review Apr/Jul/Oct; weekly NAVAC check of top/bottom 3 posts feeding next month's calendar.

## Optimisation loop
Hook test (2 hooks/month), posting-time test per platform, format test (talking head vs motion), double down on top-quartile series, retire bottom-quartile. Log every learning in 12.

## Do-not-promise
No virality promises. Organic reach potential only; paid boosts only with approved budget.
