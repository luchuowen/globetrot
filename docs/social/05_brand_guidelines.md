## Type
Archivo (display, heavy/uppercase for headlines) · IBM Plex Sans (body) · IBM Plex Mono (labels, route/ID microcopy).
## Logo use
Dark backgrounds: `docs/brand/globetrot-logo-dark.png`. Light backgrounds: `globetrot-logo-light.png`. Icon: `globetrot-icon.png` (transparent; needs light ground). Never stretch, recolour or put on busy photos without scrim.
## Visual language (RECOMMENDED)
- Night-port palette; amber used for ONE key word/number per graphic.
- Route-line motif (dotted amber arcs) as connective device.
- Mono labels like `CHINA → MOMBASA · ~22–30 DAYS (INDICATIVE)`.
- Photography/video: cinematic, Kenyan people, no third-party liveries or logos (site rule). Real footage preferred; AI/stock allowed within budget and labelled internally.
- Every graphic: 1 idea, big headline, ≤ 25 words, CTA footer (WhatsApp / site), logo bottom.
- Centre-aligned and balanced on mobile (Owen's standing preference). Fewer words.
## Voice
Precise, calm, globally fluent, unmistakably Nairobi. Plain English; light Swahili/Sheng only if client approves. No hype, no "best/cheapest", no guarantees of transit time or duty amounts — label "indicative/estimate".
