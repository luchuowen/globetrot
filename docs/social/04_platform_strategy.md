# Platform strategy
Status: RECOMMENDED. Evidence below; client approval pending (see 08).

## Evidence (researched 2026-10-30)
- DataReportal Digital 2026 Kenya (data to Oct 2025). **Ad-reach, not active users**: Facebook 17.0M · TikTok 18.4M (18+) · LinkedIn 6.3M members · Instagram 3.95M. All grew ~23–35% year on year.
  https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-kenya
- Media Council of Kenya survey (via The Star, May 2026): most-used by weekly usage — WhatsApp 20% · Facebook 18% · TikTok 15% · YouTube 12% · Instagram 8% · X 7%.
  https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/infographics/2026-05-05-which-social-media-platforms-are-kenyans-using-most
- Business Daily reports more Kenyans importing directly from China, cutting out middlemen (the SME importer audience is growing).
  https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/enterprise/kenyans-ditch-middlemen-to-import-directly-from-china-5023466
- Blueprint §1.1: competitors are slow to answer; WhatsApp is how Kenyan SME importers prefer to talk.

## Platform roles
| Platform | Decision | Why | Primary job | Audience | KPI focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp Business | CREATE · priority 1 | Where enquiries convert; most-used app | Quote conversations, Status, catalogue | all | enquiries, response time |
| Facebook | CREATE · priority 1 | Largest reach in Kenya; SMEs/individuals research here | Enquiries, trust, community, shares | SME importers, individuals | reach, clicks, messages |
| LinkedIn | CREATE · priority 1 | Corporate/manufacturer buyers + B2B credibility; org-page follows convert to trust | Authority, documents, company updates | corporates, procurement, partners | followers, engagement, site clicks |
| Instagram | CREATE · priority 2 | Visual brand, Reels reuse, young SME owners | Brand + Stories + Reels | online sellers, SMEs | reach, saves, profile visits |
| TikTok | CREATE · priority 2 | Huge Kenyan reach; young traders learn to import here | Explainer cut-downs, "how importing works" | online sellers, first-time importers | views, follows, shares |
| Google Business Profile | RECOMMEND ADD | Local search + reviews; blueprint: biggest local-SEO lever | Reviews, map pack, calls | everyone searching "freight forwarder Nairobi" | views, calls, reviews |
| YouTube | DEFER (Feb–Mar 2027) | Hosts long explainers/SEO; needs real video first | Evergreen explainers | importers researching | views, watch time |
| X, Telegram, Pinterest | SKIP | Low relevance for this audience at this budget | — | — | — |

Rule: don't spread thin. One content engine (Mon/Wed/Fri) produced once, adapted per platform (proposal).
